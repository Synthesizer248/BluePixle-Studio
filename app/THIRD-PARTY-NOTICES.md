# Third party assets

The bundled Gaai and Flipper Zero pixel image packs are sourced from [Lopaka](https://github.com/sbrin/lopaka), which includes image packs attributed to Gaai and Flipper Devices. The Lopaka repository is distributed under Apache License 2.0; a copy is included at licenses/Apache-2.0-Lopaka.txt. The asset packs are embedded in the offline editor as PNG data.
